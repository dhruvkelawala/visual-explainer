# T3 visual pages

In T3 Code, a page is shown in the thread as a T3 visual page. `html_preview` checks it, and `html_render` shows it above your reply. The reader sees it on a desktop or a phone without opening a browser.

## When

Use this path when the `html_preview` and `html_render` tools exist. Still write the page to the output directory, as the Deliver rules say, so `/fact-check` and later edits can find it. Do not open it in a browser, and do not send its path or a `localhost` link.

Slide decks and plan pages are not T3 visual pages yet. `html_render` rejects a deck's viewport units, and plan pages need the response round trip. In T3, deliver them as before: write the file, give the reader its path, and say that it opens outside T3. A video is an MP4, not a page.

## Steps

1. Write `<name>.src.html` in the Field Guide register (`field-guide.md`) and render it with `node <this skill's directory>/kit/render.mjs <name>.src.html --t3`, without `--open`. Fix every `✗` it lists. `--t3` leaves out the reader-reply bar and dialog, which T3's frame cannot show (their `vh` sizes make `html_render` refuse the page), so a T3 page has no `<ve-ask>`: ask in the reply instead.
2. Read the rendered `<name>.html` and pass its contents as `html`. Run `html_preview` with `appearance:"light"` at width 728, then with `appearance:"dark"` at 728 and at 390 (a phone). Fix every console error, clipped label and horizontal scroll in the source, render again, then preview again.
3. Run `html_render` with a short `title`, and set `height` to the `contentHeight` of the 728 preview, up to the tool's limit of 2000. On a desktop T3 fits the whole page; on a phone a longer page scrolls inside the frame.
4. In the final reply, do not announce the page, say where it is or restate it. Add only what the page does not say.

## How a Field Guide page fits T3

T3 adds a `<style id="t3-theme">` at the top of `<head>` and frames the page on the thread's background. The frame is 728px wide on a desktop and about 360px on a phone. The renderer puts `kit/theme.js` in the page's `<head>`. It finds that style and adds the `t3` class to `<html>`. The T3 block at the end of `kit/field-guide.css` then:

- leaves `body` with no background, so the thread shows through;
- removes the left padding of `<main>`, so the page's left edge lines up with the reply text, and keeps 12px on the right for the hard shadows;
- makes the section nav static, because a sticky bar never sticks in a frame as tall as the page;
- keeps the inside padding of the inverse and cobalt fields.

The kit sets `html{font-size:17px}` after T3's base sheet, so T3's 14px root does not shrink the type. The script leaves out the Light/Dark switch.

## Light or dark follows T3

T3 decides the scheme, not the system and not the switch. Inside T3's frame, `prefers-color-scheme` does not follow T3, so the script reads T3's `--background`. It sets `data-theme="dark"` when white text has more contrast than black on that colour (luminance under 0.179 by the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG), and `data-theme="light"` otherwise. When the reader switches T3's theme, T3 rewrites `#t3-theme` and the script picks again. Field Guide's light or dark tokens then apply as usual. Other registers follow only `prefers-color-scheme`, so in T3 use Field Guide.

## Rules for the frame

- Use no `vh`, `svh`, `lvh` or `dvh` units, and no `height:100%` on `html` or `body`. The frame grows to fit the page, so a height that follows the viewport grows without end. Both tools reject these units.
- Leave out `position:fixed` and `position:sticky` in your own CSS. The frame is as tall as the page, so nothing sticks.
- Give charts fixed pixel heights.
- Give a box with its own background at least 16px of padding on every side. Field Guide keeps its square corners; its fields already have this padding.
- Keep the page under 512,000 characters. Fonts and libraries load from their CDN. Local images written as absolute paths are inlined.
- A link to an `http(s)` page opens in T3's browser. Local paths and `localhost` links do not open.
